Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Rust Bot ("we," "our," or "the Bot") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Bot.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Collected Automatically

When you use the Bot, we may collect:

Data Type Purpose
User IDs To identify players, track game states, and enforce cooldowns
Server IDs To save server-specific settings and configurations
Message Content Only when you interact with commands (e.g., typing a guess)
Game Data Temporary game states (e.g., chess positions, wordle guesses)

2.2 Data NOT Collected

We do NOT collect or store:

  • ❌ Personal messages or direct messages
  • ❌ Voice data, recordings, or transcripts
  • ❌ Passwords, tokens, or sensitive credentials
  • ❌ Payment or financial information
  • ❌ IP addresses or geolocation data
  • ❌ Email addresses or phone numbers

3. How We Use Your Data

The data collected is used solely for:

  • ✅ Providing and improving the Bot's gaming functionality
  • ✅ Tracking game progress and states
  • ✅ Enforcing command cooldowns and preventing spam
  • ✅ Debugging and fixing issues

We do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell or share your data with third parties
  • ❌ Use your data for advertising or marketing
  • ❌ Transfer your data outside of the Bot's scope

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Storage

  • All data is stored temporarily and automatically deleted when a game session ends
  • Some data may be cached in memory for performance
  • No permanent or persistent user data is stored

4.2 Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data:

  • 🔒 Data is stored securely in memory
  • 🔒 No external databases or third-party storage
  • 🔒 Regular security audits and updates

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses the following third-party services:

Service Purpose
API Fetching trivia questions, game data, etc.

These services may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention

  • Game data is retained only during active gameplay
  • All data is automatically cleared when:
    • The game session ends
    • The Bot restarts
    • The game times out

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • ✅ Request deletion of your data (contact us via support server)
  • ✅ Opt-out of using the Bot at any time
  • ✅ Be informed about what data is collected

To exercise these rights, please contact us through our Support Server.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify users of any significant changes through our Support Server.
